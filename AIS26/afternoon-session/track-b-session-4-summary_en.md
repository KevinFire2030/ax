## One-Sentence Summary

Synthesia presented how video can evolve from passive content into conversational AI agents, using role-play simulations and feedback loops to help people practice difficult conversations in sales, customer support, interviews, coaching, and training.

## Core Message

The session opened with a simulation of a frustrated customer conversation. The speaker prompted an AI agent to act as an unhappy customer, allowing a sales representative to practice responding as if the situation were real. The key idea was not simply to generate answers, but to create a safe environment where people can repeatedly practice difficult workplace conversations.

The speaker emphasized that practice alone is not enough. Simulations become valuable only when they are paired with concrete feedback based on the full conversation transcript. Synthesia's role-play sessions capture the interaction and provide coaching on what the user said, how much ownership and empathy were expressed, and how specific sentences could be improved.

## Role-Play Session Structure

The role-play workflow has three main parts.

- An AI avatar performs a specific role, such as customer, interviewer, manager, or coach.
- The user conducts the conversation as if it were a real scenario.
- An AI coach analyzes the transcript and gives feedback on tone, ownership, accountability, and improved phrasing.

In the example, when a customer complained about late delivery and damaged goods, the feedback showed that simply saying "there was a logistics issue" was too generic. A stronger answer would explicitly take responsibility and state a clear action plan, such as: "I take full responsibility. Your items will be delivered by Thursday, and I will personally follow up."

## Use Cases

The speaker stressed that the feature is not limited to customer conversations. It can be used for discovery calls, cold calls, interview preparation, manager feedback, field sales training, media training, and almost any situation that requires a difficult conversation.

In an early pilot with a leading global company, users applied the feature to sales pitch preparation and technical proposal training. A particularly important result was that seven out of ten representatives returned voluntarily to repeat simulations even after completing the required program, showing that users found value in practicing for real-world situations.

## Broader Shift

The closing message was that "video can now hold conversations." Text was the first major AI unlock, voice made AI feel more human, and video adds the final layer of immersion and engagement.

These video agents can become application overlays that read context, personalized tutorial guides connected to knowledge bases, website concierge agents, online store assistants, or eventually personalized characters in movies and entertainment.

## Key Takeaways

- AI agents can move beyond information delivery and become training partners for real conversations.
- The value of simulation comes from both repetition and transcript-based feedback.
- Good coaching helps users reflect on their own conversation and improve specific wording.
- Video agents can extend into learning, sales, customer experience, web concierge flows, and entertainment.
- The next stage of real-time AI combines text, voice, face, context, and immersion.
